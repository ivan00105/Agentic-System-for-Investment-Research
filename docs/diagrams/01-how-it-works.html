How it works, by example

One real run, start to finish. You ask about a company; a coordinator and three analysts gather evidence with tools; code checks every number; a critic catches a mistake and the team fixes it; you get a report where each number links to its source.

Scroll the diagram sideways to see all of it.

How to read it

  • Every figure on this page comes from the saved trace of that run (3 Nov 2026, NVIDIA, condition D), not from a mock-up.
  • Why numbers can be trusted: the LLM never types a figure. It cites a record ID, and the host fills in the value from that record. Click a [n] in the app to see the record, e.g. [2] above.
  • Statuses: a section is cited when every citation resolves and nothing is flagged, qualified when a line still carries a flag (from the checker, or an open material objection), and abstained when it has no claims. Whether a source really supports a line is judged in the evaluation, not by the checker.
  • The correction in step 4 is the critic doing its job: the draft said the five-year operating margin peaked at 60.4%, the critic pointed to FY2025's 62.4%, and the fundamentals analyst rechecked and fixed the claim.
